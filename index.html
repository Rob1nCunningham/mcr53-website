About MCR53

Product and transformation expertise, applied with rigour.

Why MCR53

MCR53 exists because too much transformation work stalls between the strategy deck and delivery reality. We work at that gap — close enough to the detail to be useful, senior enough to change how decisions get made.

That approach is grounded in hands-on experience across two of the most heavily regulated industries there are — fintech and gambling — leading transformation work spanning compliance, payments and fraud, and retail security, with process design at the centre of all of it: the systems and rituals that make regulated change safe to ship, not just safe to approve.

How we think

Three principles that don't move

Pragmatic, not theoretical

Frameworks earn their place only if they change a decision. We start from your constraints, not a template.

Outcomes over outputs

Embedded, not distant

We work inside your teams, not from a slide deck at arm's length — so the capability stays after we leave.
